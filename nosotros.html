Our Story

A salon that feels like family.

Broadway Nails & Lashes was born from a simple idea: taking care of yourself shouldn't feel formal or rushed.

The Founder

Linda

Linda has spent more than 30 years dedicated to cosmetology, nails, makeup, and esthetics. Before opening Broadway, she already knew half the neighborhood by name — and by their hands.

She opened this salon so every client would feel at home: no rush, no being treated like a number, with the quality of a salon that knows exactly what it's doing. That includes a VIP Room available for special occasions — bachelorette parties, birthdays, afternoons with friends.
